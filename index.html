    Meal guide

    What each option on Meal Passport means. These are general descriptions. The airline decides exactly what goes into each meal.

    No fish oil. Veg, Vegan and Jain meals are to be prepared without fish oil.

    Meal types

    Veg (vegetarian)
    No meat, poultry or fish, and no fish oil. Dairy is usually included. Egg practice varies by airline.
    Vegan
    No animal products at all: no meat, fish, dairy, eggs or honey. No fish oil is used.
    Jain
    A vegetarian meal that also leaves out onion, garlic and root vegetables such as potato and carrot. Practice varies between families, so add a note if you have more specific needs. No fish oil is used.
    Halal
    Food permitted under Islamic dietary law: no pork, no alcohol, and meat from animals slaughtered in the prescribed way.
    Diabetic
    A meal planned for people with diabetes, usually lower in sugar and balanced in carbohydrates. This is a meal type, not medical advice.
    No beef
    Beef is left out. Other meats may be served.
    Regular
    The standard meal, with no dietary restriction.

    Extra needs

    No onion, garlic or root veg
    Leaves out onion, garlic and root vegetables. Already part of the Jain meal.
    Low sugar
    Reduces added sugar where the airline can.
    No egg
    Leaves out egg and egg-based dishes.
    Other (allergy or note)
    A short free-text note for anything not listed, such as an allergy. The crew can see the note. For a serious allergy, please also tell the crew in person.
    No restrictions
    Clears your extra needs.
